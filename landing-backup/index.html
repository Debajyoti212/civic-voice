From Report to Resolution

How Civic Voice Works

A seamless pipeline that routes every civic complaint to the right department within seconds.

1

📱 Citizen Reports an Issue

Open the app, snap a photo, select a category, and the system auto-detects your GPS location. Submit in under 30 seconds.

2

⚡ Smart Routing Engine

Our backend instantly classifies the issue and routes it to the correct municipal department — water, roads, electricity, sanitation, or public safety.

3

🔧 Department Takes Action

Municipal staff receive the complaint with all details, photos, and precise location. They update the status in real-time as work progresses.

4

✅ Resolution & Feedback

Citizens get notified when resolved, can verify the fix, and rate the response. Full transparency from report to resolution.
